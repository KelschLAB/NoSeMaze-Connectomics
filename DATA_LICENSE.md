A dataset may be released under different terms if its directory or associated
metadata contains an explicit data license. Those dataset-specific terms take
precedence over this default notice for that dataset.

GPL-licensed. Generated scientific data and result files are governed by the
applicable data terms unless the output itself contains GPL-covered code.

## Data availability for the associated publication

This repository's software license is separate from the manuscript's Data
Availability Statement. Any dataset that must be made available to support
verification or reproduction of the associated publication should be released
or made accessible under the conditions stated in the manuscript and/or the
corresponding data repository.

For permission requests concerning data reuse, contact the corresponding
author or relevant data owner.
